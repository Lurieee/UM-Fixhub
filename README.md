# UM-Fixhub

DESCRIPTION:
UM FixHub: Smart Campus Repair & Maintenance System

UM FixHub is a smart, centralized campus repair and maintenance platform designed to make reporting, tracking, and resolving facility issues faster, easier, and more transparent.

From broken classroom equipment and damaged facilities to electrical, plumbing, and maintenance concerns, UM FixHub connects students, faculty, staff, and maintenance personnel through one streamlined system.

🚀 Why UM FixHub?

Campus problems shouldn’t get lost in messages, paperwork, or word of mouth.

With UM FixHub, users can quickly report an issue, provide important details, and monitor its progress—from the moment a concern is submitted until it is resolved.

✨ Key Features

* 📝 Easy Issue Reporting — Submit maintenance concerns with descriptions, locations, categories, and supporting photos.
* 📊 Real-Time Status Updates — Track requests from Reported → In Review → In Progress → Resolved.
* 🛠️ Maintenance Management — Help maintenance teams organize, prioritize, and manage repair requests efficiently.
* 🔔 Notifications — Keep users informed about updates to their reported issues.
* 📈 Dashboard & Analytics — Turn maintenance data into useful insights for better campus management.
* 🔐 Role-Based Access — Provide appropriate tools and permissions for students, staff, administrators, and maintenance personnel.

🎯 Our Goal

UM FixHub aims to build a smarter, safer, and more responsive campus by transforming traditional maintenance reporting into a digital, data-driven process.

See it. Report it. Track it. Fix it.

🏫 Built for a Better Campus

Whether it’s a flickering light, a broken chair, a leaking faucet, damaged equipment, or another facility concern, UM FixHub makes sure every report has a place, a status, and a path toward resolution.
